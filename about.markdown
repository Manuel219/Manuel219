# Hi! I'm Emmanuel Obodoechina

My career in IT began in **IT Helpdesk Support**, where I developed a passion for troubleshooting technical issues, helping end users, and delivering effective solutions. This experience built a strong foundation in IT and sparked my curiosity about how networks operate and how organizations protect their systems from cyber threats.

Motivated by this curiosity, I studied **Computer Networks and Security at Birmingham City University**, where I deepened my understanding of networking and cybersecurity. I became particularly interested in how sophisticated cyberattacks occur, why organizations fall victim to them, and how security professionals can identify vulnerabilities and strengthen defenses.

Beyond my technical work, I am passionate about **knowledge sharing, teaching, and mentoring**. I have trained students and interns in my workplace, sharing practical knowledge about networking, cybersecurity, and resources to support their professional development. Helping others build their skills and confidence is something I find genuinely rewarding.

My dedication to continuous learning and practical experience has contributed to my progression into a **Network and Security Analyst** role. I hold the **CCNA, CompTIA A+, Security+, and CySA+ certifications**, and I am currently studying towards further penetration testing and ethical hacking qualifications, including CEH, to expand my cybersecurity expertise.

I am passionate about solving complex technical problems, understanding emerging cyber threats, and helping organizations maintain secure and reliable IT environments. I believe continuous learning is essential in an industry that evolves as rapidly as cybersecurity.

I am currently seeking opportunities that will allow me to apply my technical expertise and industry knowledge, take on new challenges, and continue growing as a **network security professional**, while making a meaningful contribution to protecting organizations against evolving cyber threats.
